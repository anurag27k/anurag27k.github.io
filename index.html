01 About

Turning complex problems into meaningful experiences where systems thinking meets human-centered design.

Anurag designs enterprise products across AI, SaaS, demand and supply management, healthcare, ERP and government platforms. He leads complex 0-to-1 work and shapes product strategy, bringing together product thinking, systems design, research and technical depth to turn ambiguous problems into intuitive, data-driven experiences. He works with cross-functional leaders to set product vision and steer AI-powered work, and cares about building strong teams and helping designers grow.
