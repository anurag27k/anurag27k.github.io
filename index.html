01 Currently
Designing the substrate beneath enterprise AI — the ground truth, the evaluation, the trust.

At ServiceNow's Now Assist Skill Kit team I work alongside PMs, ML engineers and applied research to design how humans curate, evaluate and trust agentic AI. Ground-truth anatomy, trajectory comparison, custom LLM metrics, LLM-as-Judge — the systems that decide what production AI is allowed to do.

Across eight years I've moved between government platforms, tax and healthcare, ERP and design systems — and the constant has been the same: heavy data, real constraints, and users who cannot afford a pretty guess.
